What the tiers mean Episodes count if they mention the term 3+ times. In each period a show is:
  • Normal with 8+ episodes.
  • Limited evidence with 3 to 7: faded and marked rough. It is placed without moving the other shows and left out of the group average.
  • Too few with 1 or 2: listed here, not placed.
Ticking Include adds a faded show to the group average. A note at the top of the page then explains what changes. Tiers by qualifying episodes n (3+ mentions): n ≥ 8 placed (bootstrap intervals); 3 ≤ n ≤ 7 "thin": v from the show's pooled per-episode spread (its 8+ cells in this bundle, else corpus-wide) / n, Gower out-of-sample placement, distance from the solid-show group with an analytic score-type interval (d_eff, r1 from the pooled within-cell covariance; methods log M4), excluded from m₋ᵢ, MDS, pairs, leans and shifts; n ≤ 2 listed only.
Include recomputes in the browser, for periods where an included show is thin: m₋ᵢ over solid + included cells (equal weights) and |μ_i − m₋ᵢ|² − (v_i + Σv_others/k²) with the analytic interval for every show. The analytic interval is near nominal 90% while the default bootstrap over-covers near zero, so "reaches zero" can flip with the method alone.

Methods

Switch "Explanations" at the top between Plain and Technical for the level of detail.

What it measures

For one term at a time, which words political podcasts use around it, and how similar or different those word choices are between shows and over time. It describes vocabulary and topics, not opinions, stance or ideology.For a target term, the distribution of its local contexts per show and period, summarised as a mean à la carte (ALC) embedding (Rodriguez, Spirling and Stewart 2023), and noise-corrected distances between those means. Vocabulary and topic, not stance.

From episodes to a dot

Episodes were transcribed automatically from public RSS feeds. Around every mention of the term, the nearby nouns, names and adjectives are turned into numbers that capture meaning; each episode with 3+ mentions is averaged, and each show-period averages its episodes. Counting episodes, not mentions, keeps very frequent shows from dominating. A show needs 8 such episodes in a period to be shown normally.Whisper medium (int8) transcripts; spaCy lemmatisation (NOUN, PROPN, ADJ); word2vec 100-d (window 5); ALC transform A learned on words with ≥ 10 uses (hold-out median cosine 0.88). Mention embedding = A × mean of ±5-token context vectors; episode = mean of its mentions (≥ 3); cell μ = mean over episodes, v = tr(cov)/n. Placed if n ≥ 8; 3-7 "thin".

The map

The map squeezes many dimensions into two, so it keeps only part of the detail. Positions are fitted once for all periods, so movement over time is comparable. The numbers in the tabs use the full detail.Classical MDS of all placed cells on noise-corrected squared distances (one joint frame); centred variant subtracts each period's equal-weight mean. Share of variation kept is shown under the map.

Measuring differences, and uncertainty

Differences are corrected for the fact that a show with few episodes looks different by chance; zero means "can't be told apart from chance". Every number has a 90% range: a range touching zero means no clear difference. Shows with few episodes are marked rough and use a formula-based range.d² = |μ_a − μ_b|² − v_a − v_b, clipped at 0; distance from the group is leave-one-out against the equal-weight mean of the others. 90% percentile bootstrap over episodes with the noise term subtracted twice per draw (conservative near zero). Thin cells and the include toggle use an analytic score-type interval from v, d_eff and r1 (simulated coverage ≈ 90%).

Words, passages and AI labels

The words that pull toward each side are clues about what to read, not proof. Passages are the words around real mentions, picked by ranking, so they show the clearest cases. AI labels on the map's edges are a model's reading of the edge words; they interpret directions on the map, not the podcasts.Word-pull: cosine ratio between the two sides' means over the union of each side's 150 nearest words (≥ 50 corpus uses). Passages: mentions ranked by distance to μ or projection on the contrast/drift direction, one per episode. AI direction interpretations: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5), prompt stored with the output, cached; only the word lists are sent.

Limits

Automatic transcripts contain errors. Before July 2022 only some conservative shows are in the data, so "the group" means those shows then. The newest period is still in progress.
