? Each dot is one show in the period shown: the average of its episodes' use of the term.
Overall similarity: closer dots use the term with more similar vocabulary. The axes are the directions that spread the shows out most; the words at each edge describe what each end of an axis leans toward (a description of a mixture, not a definition). The picture keeps only part of the variation, so read the distances in the charts below.
Relative to the pack: each period's average across the shows on the map is subtracted first, so movement shared by everyone (usually the news cycle) is removed and trails show who moved relative to the others. The shared movement itself is described under the map. Needs 3+ shows in a period.
Anchored axes: each axis is defined in advance by two sets of anchor words (e.g. diplomacy words vs military words); a show's position is how far its use of the term leans toward one set or the other. Positions mean the same thing in every period and for every term, but closeness on this map is only closeness on these two dimensions. The current axes are drafts.
Selected shows get a faint trail through their own other periods; the hollow ring marks the period change is measured from. There are no lines between different shows: closeness is shown by position, and the exact distances are in the charts below.
Labels: "On hover" (default) shows a show's full name when you hover over it, for a few seconds after; selected shows always keep theirs. Alt-click (Option-click on a Mac) a dot to pin its name, again to unpin. "All" labels every dot, hiding names that would overlap. "Zoom to selected" frames the selected shows and their trails. Scroll on the map to zoom; hold Ctrl while scrolling to scroll the page instead.
Compare shows: click a show for Show A, a second show for Show B, empty space to clear. Shift-click a show to set Show C (compared with Show A). For two unrelated pairs, use the "Second pair" menus. A second pair that shares a show with the first answers "is it closer to one or the other?".
Compare groups: pick two groups. Their shows are ringed on the map, and a diamond marks each group's average (the equal-weight average of its shows placed in that period) with its own trail. The diamond sits at the average position of those shows: exact on anchored axes, a close approximation on the other two maps. It is drawn only when all of the group's placed shows are on this map.
Trail: how many earlier periods to draw ("All periods" also shows later ones).
